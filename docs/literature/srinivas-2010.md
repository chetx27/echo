# Srinivas, N., Krause, A., Kakade, S. M., & Seeger, M. (2010)

- **Citation:** Srinivas, N., Krause, A., Kakade, S. M., & Seeger, M. (2010). Gaussian process optimization in the bandit setting: No regret and experimental design. In *Proceedings of the 27th International Conference on Machine Learning*.
- **Year:** 2010
- **Problem:** Cumulative / simple regret for GP bandit optimization.
- **Method:** GP-UCB; connections to experimental design.
- **Dataset/environment:** GP bandits and optimization benchmarks.
- **Objective:** Optimize an unknown function with confidence bounds.
- **Baseline:** Other bandit/BO methods.
- **Limitations:** The guarantee is about optimization regret, not scientific understanding.
- **Relevance to ECHO:** UCB is a planned later baseline. V0 includes EI instead, as specified for the first experiment.
- **Potential research gap:** Optimization regret can decrease while large regions of a scientific mechanism remain unsampled.
