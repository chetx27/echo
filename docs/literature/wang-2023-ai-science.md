# Wang, H., et al. (2023)

- **Citation:** Wang, H., Fu, T., Du, Y., Gao, W., Huang, K., Liu, Z., Chandak, P., Liu, S., Van Katwyk, P., Deac, A., Anandkumar, A., Bergen, K., Gomes, C. P., Ho, S., Kohli, P., Lasenby, J., Leskovec, J., Liu, T.-Y., Manrai, A., Marks, D., … Zitnik, M. (2023). Scientific discovery in the age of artificial intelligence. *Nature*, 620, 47–60.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Problem:** Survey of AI methods used across scientific domains.
- **Method:** Review; not a single algorithm.
- **Dataset/environment:** Many, via cited work.
- **Objective:** Broad: prediction, generation, design, automation of science.
- **Baseline:** n/a (review).
- **Limitations:** High-level; does not replace a targeted experimental-design review.
- **Relevance to ECHO:** Context for "AI for science." ECHO should not be written as if that field began here. V0 is a narrow sequential-design prototype, not a general scientific-discovery system.
- **Potential research gap:** The review covers many prediction/generation systems. Budget-constrained sequential *decision* evaluation with multi-metric discovery scores remains a more specific slice.
