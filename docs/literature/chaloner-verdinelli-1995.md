# Chaloner, K., & Verdinelli, I. (1995)

- **Citation:** Chaloner, K., & Verdinelli, I. (1995). Bayesian experimental design: A review. *Statistical Science*, 10(3), 273–304.
- **Year:** 1995
- **Problem:** Designing experiments to reduce posterior uncertainty or expected loss.
- **Method:** Review of Bayesian design criteria (including Shannon information, quadratic loss, linear design).
- **Dataset/environment:** Classical experimental-design settings.
- **Objective:** Decision-theoretic expected utility of a design.
- **Baseline:** Frequentist optimal design (A-, D-, E-optimality, etc.).
- **Limitations:** Often batch design for parametric models; sequential discovery of unknown functional form is not the center.
- **Relevance to ECHO:** Situates ECHO inside experimental design rather than as an unprecedented "AI scientist."
- **Potential research gap:** Sequential policies evaluated on mechanism recovery and competing hypotheses, not only on a single expected-utility criterion.
