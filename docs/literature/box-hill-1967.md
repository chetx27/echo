# Box, G. E. P., & Hill, W. J. (1967)

- **Citation:** Box, G. E. P., & Hill, W. J. (1967). Discrimination among mechanistic models. *Technometrics*, 9(1), 57–71.
- **Year:** 1967
- **Problem:** Choose experiments to discriminate among competing mechanistic models.
- **Method:** Expected change in entropy / pairwise information between model predictive distributions.
- **Dataset/environment:** Chemical-kinetic style parametric models.
- **Objective:** Model discrimination, not optimum-seeking.
- **Baseline:** Non-adaptive or one-model design.
- **Limitations:** Assumes the true model is in the candidate set; parametric forms must be specified.
- **Relevance to ECHO:** Direct ancestor of ECHO hypothesis discrimination (Experiment 2). V1 must not be described as a new discrimination criterion.
- **Potential research gap:** Whether discrimination still helps when a GP (misspecified nonparametric) is used in parallel, and how it compares to generic uncertainty on a hidden law.
